Our Origins

From origin to opportunity

We aim to build trusted sourcing relationships across major coffee-growing regions, with a focus on traceability, quality and sustainable supply. Select a region on the map or the tabs below to explore.

South America

Home to some of the world's largest Arabica producers, offering balanced acidity and consistent volumes suited to both commercial and specialty programs.

BrazilColombiaPeruEcuador

Typical Profile

Medium body, nutty-to-chocolatey notes, balanced acidity. Well suited to espresso blends and everyday roast programs.

Central America

High-altitude growing regions producing bright, clean-cupping Arabica prized by specialty roasters.

GuatemalaHondurasNicaraguaCosta Rica

Typical Profile

Bright acidity, floral and citrus notes, clean finish — favoured for single-origin and light-roast programs.

Africa

Birthplace of Arabica, offering some of the most distinctive and sought-after flavour profiles in the world.

EthiopiaUgandaKenyaTanzania

Typical Profile

Fruity, wine-like and floral notes from washed and natural processes; strong Robusta supply from Uganda.

Asia

Major volume producer of Robusta alongside distinctive processed Arabica, supporting both blends and specialty needs.

IndiaVietnamIndonesia

Typical Profile

Full body, earthy and spiced notes; Robusta with high crema for espresso and instant coffee manufacturing.
