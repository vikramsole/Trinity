Our Process

From farm to shipment

1

Sourcing

Coffee is sourced from verified growers and cooperatives across our origin regions.

2

Sampling & Cupping

Representative samples are cupped and evaluated against agreed specifications.

3

Inspection

Moisture, defect count and screen size are checked before shipment approval.

Coffee is packed to the agreed format, from bulk bags to retail-ready units.

5

Shipment

Documentation is prepared and shipment coordinated to the destination port.
